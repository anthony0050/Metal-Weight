Description 说明
This application calculates theoretical metal weight based on entered dimensions and material density.

Actual weight may vary due to material tolerance, thickness variation, welding, fabrication and other manufacturing factors.

本应用根据输入尺寸及材料密度计算金属理论重量。

实际重量可能因材料公差、板厚误差、焊接、加工及其他制造因素而有所不同。
